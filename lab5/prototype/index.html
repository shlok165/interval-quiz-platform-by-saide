Q4 · short · 1 pt

The queued edit stays pending locally. It is flushed on the next save tick or the next answer change — nothing acknowledged by the server is ever re-requested (idempotent via revision).

Sticky note — what “nothing is lost” means: the acknowledged state is the contract. If the app dies here, reopening shows exactly the server’s last-acknowledged snapshot.
